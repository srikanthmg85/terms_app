## Who we are
CareLog is a small pilot project run by **[your full name]** from **[city, country]**. It is not a hospital or a clinic, and it does not give medical advice. You can contact us at **[email address]**.

## What CareLog does
CareLog is a WhatsApp assistant that asks you about a health problem (yours, or someone you care for), writes down your answers, and prepares a summary that a doctor can read to see how things change over time. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace a doctor.

## What we collect
- **Your WhatsApp phone number**, so we can reply to you.
- **What you write or say** to the assistant, including symptoms, when they started, how bad they are, medicines you mention, readings you share (for example a blood sugar number), and the name or relationship of the person it is about.
- **Voice messages.** If you send a voice message, it is turned into text. We do not keep the audio file. [Check this is true for your setup before keeping this sentence.]
- **Your answer to the consent question** (YES or NO) and when you gave it.
- **Technical records** such as the time of each message, and which questions were asked.

We do not ask for your address, ID number, bank details, or photographs.

## Why we collect it
Only to run the pilot: to ask relevant questions, to prepare the summary for the doctor you choose to share it with, and to find and fix problems with the assistant.

## Who sees it, and where it goes
- **The pilot team** ([your name], and any doctor taking part in the pilot): the doctor sees a summary of your answers so they can follow your health between visits.
- **Service providers that process your messages for us:**
  - **Groq** (an AI service, in the United States) reads your messages and turns voice messages into text.
  - **Meta / WhatsApp** carries the messages between you and us. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that part.
  - [**Sarvam AI or Google**, if voice replies are switched on: the text of the assistant's reply is sent to create the spoken version. Remove this line if you do not use voice replies.]
- We do **not** sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

## Your choices
- **Stop at any time** by sending the word **stop** to the assistant, and it will send you no more messages.
- **Ask us to see, correct or delete** what we hold about you by writing to [email address]. We will do this within **[number]** days. [Make sure you can actually do this: deleting a patient's records is not automatic yet.]
- You can withdraw consent at any time. This does not affect messages already shared with a doctor before you withdrew, unless you ask us to delete those too and the doctor agrees.

## Children and people you care for
If you use CareLog for a child or another person, you should be their parent or guardian, or have their permission. Answers are stored as information about that person.

## Emergencies
CareLog is **not** for emergencies and does not monitor your messages all the time. If you or someone else may be in danger, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital.
